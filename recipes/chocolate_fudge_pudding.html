Chocolate Fudge Pudding

Back

Ingredients

    Cake:
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150 g flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 cup milk
    • Sauce:
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 1/4 cup boiling water

Instructions

    Cake:
  1. Cream the butter and sugar.
  2. Add eggs and vanilla.
  3. Add flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt alternating with the milk.
  4. Pour batter into 8x8 pan.
    5. Sauce:
  5. Mix the boiling water with the brown sugar and the cocoa powder. Pour mixture slowly over cake.
  6. Bake for 35 mins in 175ºC, 160ºC fan, or 350ºF.
