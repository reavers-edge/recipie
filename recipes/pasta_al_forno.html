Pasta al Forno

Back

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 onion
  • 225 g ground pork
  • 170 g italian sausage
  • 750 ml chopped tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 500 g rigatoni pasta
  • 2 cups water (from the pasta)
  • 350 g mozzarella cheese
    • or
  • 350 g pecorino romano cheese
    • or
  • 60 g parmesan

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Dice the [onion] and cut the [sausage].
    2. Recipe
  2. Heat the olive in a large skillet over medium heat. Add finely chopped onion and a pinch of salt. Fry for 5 minutes or until just taking on some colour.
  3. Add ground pork and sausage. Increase the heat and cook until browned, about 6-9 minutes.
  4. When the meat has browned and is well cooked, add the tomato puree, ½ cup water, salt and pepper. Stir well and when the sauce starts bubbling, turn the heat down to low, partly cover with the lid and simmer for 30 minutes.
  5. In the meantime, bring a large pot of water to the boil. Salt well (2 or 3 tablespoons of salt) and add the pasta. Check the package instructions and drain the pasta 3 or 4 minutes before being completely cooked. Reserve 2 cups of the cooking water.
  6. Cut the mozzarella cheese into cubes or roughly chop.
  7. Mix together really well adding more cooking water if it looks dry. There should be sauce at the bottom of the dish. Most likely you'll need about 1 cup of water but it could be up to 1 ½ cups. This will keep the pasta moist and make it creamy.
  8. Tip into a baking dish 8 x 12 inches (20x30cm/12 cup capacity) and cover with remaining cheeses.
  9. Bake 350ºF/180ºC for 20-25 minutes.
