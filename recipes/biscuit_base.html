Ingredients

  • 250 g biscuits
  • 115 g butter (melted)

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together, and press into an 8x8 pan.
    2. Options
  2. Preheat the oven to 180°/350°F.
  3. Bake for 10 minutes.
    • or
  4. If you don't want to bake the crust for your no-bake recipe, simply refrigerate for at least an hour before moving on.
