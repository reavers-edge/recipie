Pineapple Chicken

Back

Ingredients

    Sauce
  • 200 ml pineapple juice
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce, light
  • 2 Tbsp cornflour
  • 4 Tbsp lime juice
  • 2/3 Tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp sriracha chili sauce
    • Chicken
  • 4 chicken thights
  • pinch salt
  • pinch white pepper
  • 2 Tbsp cornflour
  • 2 Tbsp rapeseed oil
  • 2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 2 Tbsp shaoxing rice wine
  • 7 pineapple rings
  • 1 red pepper
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 cashew nuts
    • To Serve
  • rice
  • fresh coriander

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Cut the [red pepper and pineapple rings,] keeping the juice. Cut the [spring onions. Optionally roast the cashew nuts.] (for the end) Cut the [chicken thighs] into bite sized pieces,
    2. Sauce
  2. Whisk together all the sauce ingredients in a small jug and set aside.
    3. Pineapple Chicken
  3. Put the chicken in a bowl and season with salt and black pepper. Add the cornflour, mix well to coat and set aside.
  4. Heat a wok over a high heat until smoking. Add the rapeseed oil and chilli flakes and fry for a few seconds. Add the chicken and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes. As it starts to turn opaque, add the rice wine and cook for 2-3 minutes, or until chicken is cooked through.
  5. Add the pineapple and red pepper and cook for 30 seconds. Pour in the sauce, bring to the boil and simmer until the volume has reduced by half and the sauce is slightly sticky and thick. Add the spring onion and cashews (if using) and cook for 20 seconds, stirring well.
    6. To serve
  6. Transfer the chicken to a serving plate, garnish with the fresh coriander and serve immediately with the rice.
