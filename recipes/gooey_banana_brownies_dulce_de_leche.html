Gooey Banana Brownies with Dulce de Leche

Back

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • 2 cup chocolate
  • 2 (250 g) bananas
  • 6 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cup sugar
  • 230 g flour
  • 1 tsp salg
  • 397 g condensed milk (or dulce de leche)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven 185ºC, 170ºC fan, or 360ºF. Grease and line a 30 x 20 cm brownie pan.
  2. In a medium size bowl melt butter and chocolate. If using a microwave to melt the butter and chocolate, check and stir in 30 second bursts to not to burn the chocolate.
  3. Leave the chocolate mixture to the side to cool slightly.
  4. In a separate large bowl beat eggs and sugar until combined.
  5. Mix mashed bananas into the chocolate mixture, mix well.
  6. Then add the banana chocolate mixture to the beaten eggs and sugar, mix well.
  7. Sift flour into the brownie mix and add salt.
  8. Pour brownie mix into the greased and lined tine.
  9. Dollop dulce de leche over the top of the brownie mix. Use a knife to 'swizzle' the dulce de leche through the brownie slightly.
  10. Place in the oven and bake for around 35 minutes until it is cooked through. The brownie will still be a little jiggly in the middle, but solid. It will firm up on cooling Remove and allow to cool before cutting into desired sized portions.
