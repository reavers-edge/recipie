Almond Cream Cheese Bar

Back

Ingredients

    Bar:
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup cream cheese
  • 300 g icing sugar
  • 1/2 tsp almond extract
  • 1/4 cup almonds, sliced (toasted)
  • 1 Tbsp milk
    • Topping:
  • 1 cup chocolate
  • 1 Tbsp vegetable shortening (optional)

Instructions

    Base:
  1. See ratios --- Mix the ingredients together, and press into a 8x8 pan.
    2. Recipe:
  2. Beat butter & cream cheese with mixer on medium speed until blended. Gradually add icing sugar, extract & almonds, beating until blended (mixture will be stiff).
  3. Press mixture into a 8x8 in pan. Freeze 1 hour until cold and firm.
    4. Topping:
  4. Melt chocolate as package directs, and then stir in shortening. Let stand at room temperature 5 minutes, or until lukewarm.
  5. Pour chocolate mixture onto of the bar. Place into the fridge for 1 hour to firm up.
