Magic Cookie Bars

Back

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup graham wafer crumbs
    • [11 biscuits]
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 397 g condensed milk
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 cup butterscotch chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350ºF (325ºF for glass baking pan). 13x9-inch baking pan.
  2. Melt the butter and combine the graham cracker crumbs. Press crumb mixture firmly on bottom the pan.
  3. Pour the sweetened condensed milk evenly over the crust.
  4. Sprinkle the chocolate chips and butterscotch chips evenly over the top. Press down gently.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until the edges are lightly browned. Remove from oven and let cool completely.
  6. Once cooled, cut into bars and serve. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week.
