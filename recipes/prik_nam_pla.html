Prik Nam Pla

Back

Ingredients

  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 6 red chillies
  • 1/2 lime
  • 4 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 Tbsp lime juice

Instructions

  1. Thinly slice the [garlic about 2mm thick. Slice the chilies about 3mm think. Slice the lime into 2 wedges].
  2. Pour the fish sauce into a small bowl.
  3. Add the sugar and stir until dissolved.
  4. Add the garlic, chilies, lime juice, and lime slices if using and mix.
  5. Let sit for at least 5 min to infuse.
