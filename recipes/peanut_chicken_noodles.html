Peanut Chicken Noodles

Back

Ingredients

    Sauce
  • 120 g peanut butter
  • 6 Tbsp soy sauce, dark
  • 4 Tbsp water (boiling)
  • 2 green chilli
  • 1 red chilli
    • Chicken noodles
  • 250 g egg noodles
  • 2 tsp vegetable oil
  • 500 g frozen veg
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 3 cup chicken

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Dice the [garlic cloves and chillies.]
    2. Noodles Put the noodles in a large bowl and cover with boiling water. Set aside for 10-15 minutes. Alternatively cook according to the packet instructions. Sauce
  2. To make the sauce, mix the peanut butter with 3 tablespoons soy sauce using a whisk or a fork in a small bowl. When it has mixed together, add the rest of the soy sauce. When all combined, add boiling water. Set aside.
    • The sauce should come together over time.

    Chicken noodles
  3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a high heat. Add the stir-fry vegetables, garlic and chicken. Cook for 5-7 minutes until the vegetables are softened and the chicken is heated through.
  4. Check that the noodles are cooked through and drain. Add the noodles to the pan, and add the peanut sauce. Give everything a good stir until combined. Serve immediately.
