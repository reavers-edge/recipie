Blueberry Costco Muffins

Back

Ingredients

    Dry ingredients
  • 400 g flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salg
  • 1/3 tsp baking soda
    • Wet ingredients
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup buttermilk - link
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 1/3 cup yoghurt
  • 70 g butter
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
    • Additions
  • 1 1/2 cup blueberries

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and generously butter a 6-cup jumbo muffin tin. Set aside.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt and baking soda.
  3. In a separate large mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs, buttermilk, oil, yogurt, butter, lemon zest and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Combine the wet and dry ingredients until it just comes together (don't overmix), then gently fold in the blueberries.
  5. Divide the batter evenly among the muffin cups (the batter will reach to top or come close - this is fine).
  6. Bake on a middle rack in the oven for 35-40 minutes, until golden on top and a skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
  7. Let cool in the pan for 15 minutes before removing - serving warm or at room temperature.
  • These are best eaten the day they are made but you can store completely cooled leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days.
