Butter Paneer Curry

Back

Ingredients

  • 100 ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 onion
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 cm ginger root
  • 2 tsp fenugreek, ground
  • 2 Tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp tumeric
  • 1/4 tsp chilli powder
  • 150 g tomato purée
  • 500 ml water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp sugar
  • 100 ml créme fraîche
  • 250 g frozen peas
  • 500 g paneer
  • 1 Tbsp butter
    • To serve
  • fresh coriander
  • rice

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Finely chop the [onion and garlic, peel and grate the ginger].
  2. [Dry spices].
    3. Recipe
  3. Heat a pan to a high heat, then add a dash of the oil. When the oil is hot, add the cumin seeds, frying until they're almost black. Turn the heat down to medium-low, then add the rest of the oil, followed by the onion, garlic and ginger. Cook until the onions have turned golden-brown and sweet.
  4. Add the fenugreek, garam masala, turmeric and chilli powder (if using). And then the tomato purée. Allow to cook for a couple of minutes on a high heat, making sure that the tomato purée is beginning to cook through a little.
  5. Pour in 500ml/18fl oz water. Add the salt and sugar, then bring to the boil and remove from the heat. After a moment or so, stir in the crème fraîche.
  6. Return the pan to the heat, add the peas and paneer, and simmer on medium for around 10 minutes, or until the cheese has softened.
  7. Turn off the heat and gently stir in the butter.
    8. To serve
  8. Garnish with freshly coriander and serve with rice.
