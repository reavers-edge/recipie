Peanut Butter Bar

Back

Ingredients

    Bar:
  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 3/4 cup flour
    • Topping:
  • 1 cup chocolate
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter

Instructions

    Bar
  1. In a medium bowl, mix together peanut butter, honey, and flour until well combined.
  2. Press the mixture into a greased 8x8 inch pan.
    3. Topping:
  3. In a microwave-safe bowl, melt chocolate and peanut butter together, stirring every 30 seconds until smooth.
  4. Pour the chocolate mixture over the peanut butter layer and spread evenly.
  5. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour or until firm. Cut into bars and serve. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.
