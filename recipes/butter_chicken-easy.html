Butter chicken, Easy

Back

Ingredients

    Cake
  • 3/4 cup butter
  • 1 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 (~90 g) banana pudding mix
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/3 cup canola oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 3/4 cup flour
  • 3 Tbsp Cornflour
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
    • Cream cheese glaze
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 1/4 cup cream cheese
  • 2 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 dash salt

Instructions

    Cake:
  1. Preheat the oven to 325ºF. Grease and flour a standard Bundt pan.
  2. Cream together the butter & sugar until light & fluffy. Add in the banana pudding mix and mix to dissolve the pudding mix.
  3. Beat in the eggs, one at a time, making sure each is thoroughly incorporated.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, cornstarch, baking powder & salt.
  5. In a separate bowl, combine the milk, oil & vanilla extract.
  6. Starting with the dry ingredients, alternate pouring a third of the wet and dry ingredients into the bowl, incorporating thoroughly after each addition. You will end on the wet ingredients. Mix well after the last addition.
  7. Pour the cake batter into the prepared Bundt pan and bake for 50-60 minutes, or until a tester inserted into the centre of the cake comes out clean.
  8. Invert the cake onto a baking rack to cool.
    9. Cream cheese glaze:
  9. Mix together the glaze ingredients until smooth & drizzle over the cake, once it's completely cool.
  10. Top with fresh banana slices right before serving and enjoy!
