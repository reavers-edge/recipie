Butter chicken, Easy

Back

Ingredients

    Chicken
  • 4 chicken thighs
  • 6 cardamon pods
  • pinch fenugreek, ground
    • Marianade
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp cumin, ground
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp paprika, smoked
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
    • Sauce
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 15 g ginger root
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp cumin, ground
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp tumeric
  • 1 tsp fenugreek, ground
  • pinch red pepper flakes
  • 1 Tbsp sugar
  • 2 Tbsp tomato purée
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 150 ml double cream
  • pinch salt
    • To serve
  • rice
  • naan bread

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Finely grate the [garlic cloves and ginger root]. Cut the chicken thighs into bite sized pieces.
  2. [Marinade dry spices]. [Sauce dry spices till turmeric]. [Fenugreek and red pepper flakes].
    3. Recipe
  3. Preheat the oven to 220C/200C Fan/Gas 7.
    4. Marianade
  4. Mix all the marinade ingredients in a small ovenproof dish (ideally cast iron) or a small roasting tin. Add the chicken and coat thoroughly in the marinade. Roast for 10 minutes.
    5. Sauce
  5. While the chicken is cooking, make the sauce. Heat the buttern in a medium non-stick frying pan. Cook the garlic and ginger over a low heat for 1-2 minutes, or until softened but not coloured, stirring constantly. Add the garam masala, cumin, coriander and turmeric and cook for 30 seconds, stirring.
  6. Sprinkle over the fenugreek and chilli flakes and cook for a few seconds more, then add the sugar and tomato purée. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring constantly.
  7. Add the cold water and the cream and bring to a simmer. Season with salt, to taste.
  8. Stir the chicken into the creamy sauce. Return to the oven for a further 10 minutes, or until the chicken is tender and the sauce is bubbling.
  9. Just before the chicken is done, roughly bash the cardamom pods in a pestle and mortar and remove the husks. Grind the seeds to a powder. Sprinkle the ground cardamom and remaining fenugreek over the butter chicken just before serving.
