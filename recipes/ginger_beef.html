Ginger Beef

Back

Ingredients

    Beef
  • 450 g thin cut beef steaks or flank steak or sirloin
    • Batter
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 Tbsp corn starch
  • 1 Tbsp rapeseed oil
    • Sauce
  • 1/2 Tbsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/6 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/6 cup soy sauce, dark
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp red chinese vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp shaoxing rice wine (chinese cooking wine)
  • 2 Tbsp plum sauce
    • Vegetables
  • 1/2 carrot
  • 1/4 green pepper
  • 1 Tbsp ginger root
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 Tbsp sesame seeds, toasted
  • 1 Tbsp rapeseed oil
    • Garnish
  • seasame seeds, toasted
  • spring onions
  • rice

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Julienne the [carrot, thinly slice the green pepper, and mince the garlic and ginger root]. Slice the [beef] against the grain into strips.
    2. Batter
  2. Combine the eggs, water and cornstarch batter ingredients and coat the beef.
  3. On medium, add the oil then fry the beef until crispy, about 7-10 minutes. Set aside. (Deep fry)
    4. Sauce
  4. Add the sauce ingredients except plum sauce, in a bowl and whisk together. Set aside.
    5. Vegetables
  5. On medium-low add the oil and fry the garlic, carrots, green pepper, sesame seeds and ginger for about 3 minutes.
  6. Add the sauce mix. Bring to a boil for 1-2 minutes and then reduce to medium, cook until reduced for 5-7 minutes. Add plum sauce, stir and cook for 1 additional minute.
    7. Assembly
  7. Add beef to sauce, stir to coat.
    8. Garnish
  8. Serve over white or basmati rice. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds.
