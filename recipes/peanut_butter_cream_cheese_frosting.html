Peanut Butter Cream Cheese Frosting

Back

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1 cup golden corn syrup
  • 1 3/4 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 1/3 cup oats
  • 2 cup peanuts, chopped

Instructions

  1. Prepare a 10x6in pan with spray or butter.
  2. In a saucepan, add the peanut butter & corn syrup and bring to a low boil, stirring well, then remove from heat.
  3. Add chocolate chips, oats, & peanuts. Mix well until chocolate is all melted.
  4. Pour in to pan. Chill to harden. Remove for 5 mins, then slice into bars.
