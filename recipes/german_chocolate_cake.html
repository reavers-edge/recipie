German Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

    Cake
  • 130 g flour
  • 40 g cocoa powder
  • 180 g sugar
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp coffee powder
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 egg
  • 90 g sour cream
  • 1/4 cup buttermilk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup coffee
    • Coconut pecan filling
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/4 can evaporated milk
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup coconut, flakes
  • 1/4 cup pecans, chopped

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C). Grease one 8x8 in cake pans, line with parchment paper rounds, then grease the parchment paper.
    2. Cake:
  2. Whisk the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, baking soda, baking powder, salt, and espresso powder together in a large bowl. Set aside.
  3. Whisk the oil, eggs, sour cream, buttermilk, and vanilla together until combined.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients, add the hot water or coffee, and whisk or beat it all until the batter is completely combined.
  5. Bake for 21-25 minutes. Cool completely in the pan.
    6. Coconut pecan filling:
  6. Combine butter, brown sugar, egg yolks, and evaporated milk in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Whisk occasionally as the mixture comes to a low boil. Once boiling, whisk constantly until the mixture thickens, about 5 minutes.
  7. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla, toasted pecans, and coconut. Allow to cool completely before layering in cake. It will thicken even more as it cools.
  8. Spread onto cooled cake
