Buttery Almond Pound Cake

Back

Ingredients

    Cake
  • 180 g strong flour
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 1/4 cup butter, softened
  • 1 1/4 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tsp almond extract
  • 1/2 cup milk
    • Glaze
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 1 Tbsp milk
  • 2 tsp almond extract
  • 2 Tbsp almonds, sliced

Instructions

    Cake
  1. Do not preheat oven. Lightly grease and flour one-piece tube pan.
  2. Cream butter & sugar until light and fluffy. Add and mix eggs one at a time with the almond extract.
  3. Add flour, baking powder & salt, alternating with milk. until just combined. Do not over mix.
  4. Add batter to the pan. Bake in a 160ºC, 145ºC fan, or 325ºF, in an oven that has not been pre-heated. (This will give you a loftier cake and a nicer crust. Do not open the oven door). Bake for 70-80 minutes or until a toothpick comes out the centre clean.
  5. Let cool in the pan on a wire rack for 30 minutes and then turn out on the rack and turn right side up. Let cool for 3 hours.
    6. Glaze:
  6. Mix together icing sugar, milk and almond extract for the glaze and pour over the cake. Sprinkle with almonds before glaze is dry.
