Instructions

    Preparation:
  1. Mince the garlic (note 7 garlic cloves) and ginger (8 cm). Dice the onion. Mix spices.
    2. Instructions:
  2. Preheat the grill to its highest setting.
  3. Combine all the marinade ingredients and use the mixture to coat the chicken.
  4. Grill the chicken until it is charred, about 5–6 minutes on each side. It’s fine if it’s not fully cooked through at this stage.
  5. Heat the butter, oil and add cumin seeds, cinnamon sticks, cloves and star anise.
  6. Once the cumin seeds start to sizzle, add the onions.
  7. PCook the onions until they are browned, about 3–4 minutes, then add the ginger and garlic.
  8. Cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds, then add the spices and tomato paste. Continue cooking for 5 minutes, or until the paste has darkened.
  9. Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer over low heat for 20–30 minutes. Add the chicken along with any cooking liquid, the cream, and the fenugreek. Stir well and cook for 5 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked./li>
  10. Taste and adjust the seasoning as needed. Garnish with coriander and serve with rice and fresh naan.
