Orange Chicken Traybake

Back

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thights w/ bone
  • 2 Tbsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp soy sauce, dark
  • 1 Tbsp hot chilli sauce
  • 1 Tbsp rice wine vinegar
  • 1/2 orange
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 3 1/2 cm ginger root
  • pinch salt
  • pinch pepper
  • 1/2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 sweet potato
  • 100 g tenderstem broccoli
  • 90 g snap peas
  • 1 spring onion
    • Garnish
  • spring onions
  • cashew nuts
  • fresh coriander
  • red chilli

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Zest and juice the [orange, chop the garlic cloves, peel and grate the ginger root.] Peel and cut the [sweet potato.] Cut the ends off the [snap peas and broccoli, thinly slice the spring onions.]
    2. Recipe
  2. Heat the oven to 200C/180C Fan/Gas 6..
  3. In a bowl, mix together the orange zest and juice, honey, soy sauce, chilli sauce, rice wine vinegar, garlic, ginger and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  4. Make a few cuts in the chicken thighs with a sharp knife. Rub the oil over the chicken skin and put the thighs skin-side down in a hot roasting tin set over a high heat. Sear for 5-6 minutes, until the skin is golden, then turn the thighs over.
  5. Spoon some of the glaze over each thigh and scatter the sweet potatoes around the chicken.
  6. Roast for 20 minutes, then baste with more glaze and cook for another 15 minutes, until the chicken is dark golden and cooked through.
  7. Add the broccoli and sugar snap peas to the tin, pour over the remaining glaze and toss to coat. Sprinkle over the spring onions and return to the oven for a final 15 minutes, until the vegetables are tender.
    8. Garnish
  8. Scatter with spring onions, chilli, cashews and coriander (if using) to serve.
Double the glaze and add more vegetables to make a larger traybake. You can also use chicken drumsticks or boneless chicken thighs instead of bone-in thighs.
