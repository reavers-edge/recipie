Ingredients

    Lasagna
  • 12 sheets lasagne pasta
  • 454 g mozzarella cheese
  • 227 g parmesan
    • Marinara
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 120 ml red wine
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 800 g canned whole tomatoes
  • 12 fresh basil
  • pinch salt
    • Bechamel Sauce
  • 710 ml milk
  • 60 g butter
  • 30 g flour
  • pinch salt
  • pinch white pepper
    • Meat Sauce
  • 454 g beef mince
  • 454 g Italian sausage
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp fennel seeds
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 onion
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 sauce (from above)

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Shred the [cheeses]. Mince [[both garlics]]. Chop the basil leaves and onion. Remove the casings from the sausage. Note red wine.
  2. Mix the [marinara square.] Mix the [meat sauce fennel seeds and red pepper flakes.]
    3. Recipe Marinara Sauce
  3. In a large skillet, heat the oil over a medium-high flame and add the minced garlic and sauté for 2 minutes.
  4. Reduce the heat to medium and add the wine (optional), tomato paste, sugar, dried oregano and salt and red pepper flakes, sauté for 2 minutes.
    • If cooking the marinara low and slow (3-4 hour simmer), omit the tomato paste, as the sauce will thicken on its own. I use it when time is tight.
  5. Add the tomatoes, stir and simmer until reduced slightly, about 20 minutes. In last 10 minutes of simmering, add the basil and stir. Season the sauce with more salt to taste.
  6. At this point you could leave it chunky or for a smooth sauce you could use a hand blender and puree or put in a blender and blend until smooth. Set aside.
    7. Bechamel Sauce (white sauce)
  7. Warm the milk in a saucepan or microwave in measuring cup.
  8. In a different medium sauce pan melt equal parts butter. Then the flour with the salt and white pepper.
  9. Whisk together and allow to simmer on low to cook the flour 1-2 minutes. The lightest (white color) roux will be slightly puffed.
  10. Gradually add the warm milk and whisk to incorporate until a smooth sauce forms on low heat. Cook several minutes, sauce will thicken. Set aside.
    11. Meat Sauce
  11. In a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat, add olive oil. Add the fennel seeds and red pepper . Saute for 30 seconds and add the onion. Continue cooking on medium-high heat until the onion is soft, 2 minutes. Add the garlic, stirring constantly for 30 seconds.
  12. Add the ground beef and sausage and brown meat over medium-high heat, breaking the meat apart into pieces with spoon. When cooked drain excess grease and transfer to food processor. Pulse several times until crumbly and nearly pureed.
  13. Return mixture to the skillet or Dutch oven and add the Marinara Sauce (recipe above). Stir to combine the meat and marinara sauce.
  14. Cover and allow the sauce to simmer for about 20 minutes on low heat, stirring occasionally. Add 1 cup of water if it gets too thick and continue cooking to reduce.
    15. Lasagna Noodles
  15. In a 9x13″ baking dish add the dry lasagna noodles and cover with hot water. Allow to rest for 20 minutes. Drain into a large colander.
    16. Assembly
  16. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease the 9x13″ baking dish with cooking spray.
    • We will be making 3-4 layers of lasagna, so divide accordingly.
  17. Start by spreading some of the meat sauce on bottom of greased dish. Layer on top with 3 lasagna noodles or more to cover bottom.
  18. Spread more meat sauce over the noodles, followed by the Bechamel Sauce (recipe above), then mozzarella and parmesan cheeses, spreading all to the edges.
  19. Repeat this 3-4 time to use up all noodles, sauces and ending with cheeses on top. See below if freezing.
  20. Bake uncovered for 30 minutes until top is golden brown and cheeses are bubbling, unless it is getting too brown and then it should be covered with foil. Allow the lasagna to rest for 15 minutes before cutting and serving.
    21. Bake Ahead / Freeze / Bake
    • Cover and freeze at this point if cooking later. You should not cook lasagna before freezing it: freezing lasagna before baking helps it retain its texture and prevents it from becoming soggy.
  21. Preheat the oven to 350°F and cover the lasagna with aluminum foil in an oven-safe dish. Bake for about 30 minutes if thawed, or until the lasagna is hot and the sauce is bubbling. If the lasagna is frozen, it may take up to 90 minutes to reheat.
