White Chocolate Amaretto Cake

Back

Ingredients

    Cake
  • 1 pkg yellow cake mix
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 pkg (94g) instant white chocolate pudding mix
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1/2 cup amaretto liqueur
  • 1/4 tsp almond extract
    • Glaze
  • 3/4 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup amaretto liqueur
    • Topping
  • 3/4 cup almonds sliced (toasted)

Instructions

    Cake
  1. Preheat oven to 175ºC, 160ºC fan, or 350ºF. Lightly grease and flour a 10 inch Bundt pan.
    2. Toffee
  2. Combine cake mix, eggs, pudding mix, cold water, oil, amaretto & almond extract. Blend well.
  3. Pour batter into prepared pan. Bake for 45 minutes - 1 hour, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre of the cake comes out clean.
  4. Remove cake from oven, and use an ice pick or skewer to make as many holes as possible into the cake.
    5. Glaze
  5. Combine butter, sugar, water & amaretto in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, and continue to boil for 10 minutes, stirring constantly.
  6. Apply glaze while cake is still warm. Slowly and patiently drizzle glaze over cake, including the edges and centre of Bundt pan. Allow cake to cool in the pan for at least 2 hours.
    7. Topping
  7. Lightly toast slivered almonds in the oven. This will take 5-10 minutes. Stir frequently and be careful not to burn.
