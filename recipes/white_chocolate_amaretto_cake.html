White Chocolate Amaretto Cake

Back

Ingredients

    Toffee
  • 3/4 cup almonds, sliced
  • 1/4 cup almonds, sliced
    • both toasted
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/4 tsp salt
    • Topping:
  • 200 g chocolate
  • 1/4 cup almonds, sliced (from before)

Instructions

    Base:
  1. In an 8x8 in pan, sprinkle 3/4 cup of the almonds on the bottom of the pan.
    2. Toffee:
  2. Combine butter, sugar, and salt in a large, heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium heat.
  3. Stir constantly in one direction until butter is melted, about 5 minutes.
  4. Once the butter has melted bring mixture to a boil, stirring often. The mix will turn a bubbly brown colour. Mix until the temperature reaches 132ºC-143ºC for soft, or 149ºC-154ºC for hard, on a candy thermometer, 15-20 minutes.
    5. Topping:
  5. Sprinkle the chocolate on top. Let sit for a minute or so until melted, then spread the chocolate around carefully. Sprinkle the remaining almonds, and gently press them into the chocolate.
  6. Cool completely, and the break apart into chunks with a sharp knife.
  7. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
