Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 125 ml maple syurp
  • 2 Tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300 g oats
  • 100 g almonds, sliced
  • 50 g pecans, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseen
  • 100 g dried fruit

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 150C/fan 130C/gas 2.
  2. Mix the oil, maple syrup, honey and vanilla in a large bowl. Tip in all the remaining ingredients, except the flaxseed and dried fruit, and mix well.
  3. Tip the granola onto two baking sheets and spread evenly. Bake for 15 mins,
  4. Then mix in the flaxseed and dried fruit, and bake for 10-15 mins more.
  5. Remove and scrape onto a flat tray to cool. Stir to break up the chuncks as it cools.
  • The granola can be stored in an airtight container for up to a month.
