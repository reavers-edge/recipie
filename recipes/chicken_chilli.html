Chicken Chilli

Back

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp paprika, smoked
  • 15 g fresh corander stalks
  • 4 chicken thighs
  • 800 g kidney beans, tin
  • 250 g passata
  • 200 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 24 g chocolate
  • pinch pepper
    • To serve
  • rice
  • sour cream
  • fresh coriander leaves

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Slice the [onions], finely chop the [garlic. Take the coriander and separate the leaves from the stalks, chop the stalks. Mix with the dry spices.] Drain and rinse the [beans]. Cut the [chicken thighs] in to bite sized pieces.
    2. Chicken
  2. Heat the oil in a saucepan and gently fry the onion for about 10 minutes, or until softened.
  3. Add the garlic, oregano, bay leaf, cumin, chilli powder, paprika and coriander stalks and continue to cook on a low heat for a minute.
  4. Add the chicken, beans, passata, stock, cider vinegar and salt. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat and cook gently for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir in the chocolate and cook for another 5 minutes. Taste and season with salt and pepper, if desired.
    5. Chicken
  5. Scatter over the coriander leaves and serve with rice and a dollop of soured cream.
