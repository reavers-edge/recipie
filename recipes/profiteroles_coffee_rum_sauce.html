Profiteroles with Coffee Rum Sauce

Back

Ingredients

    Puffs
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 2 cup water
  • 300 g flour
  • 8 eggs
    • Cream
  • 500 ml heavy cream
  • 4 Tbsp coffee
  • 1 tsp sugar
    • Coffee Rum Sauce
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 1/2 cup coffee
  • 2 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 3 Tbsp coffee
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 2 Tbsp rum

Instructions

    Puffs
  1. Preheat oven to 400ºF.
  2. Bring the water to a boil in a medium-sized sauce pan. Add salt & butter and stir until the butter has melted. Reduce the heat. Add the flour and beat the mixture until it comes away from the sides of the pan and forms a smooth ball in the centre.
  3. Remove from heat and let cool for a few minutes. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well. Using a spoon, shape the puffs and place them on a lightly greased cookie sheet.
  4. Bake for 8 minutes, then reduce heat to 350ºF and bake for 10-12 minutes.
  5. Remove puffs from the oven and let cool.
    6. Cream:
  6. Whip the heavy cream. Slowly add the cold coffee to the whipped cream, and fold in well. Sweeten to taste.
    7. Coffee Rum Sauce:
  7. Slowly melt sugar in a saucepan over low heat, stirring constantly. Gradually add strong coffee, continuing to stir constantly until the sugar is completely dissolved.
  8. In a small bowl, stir the cornstarch into the cold coffee and combine with heated mixture.
  9. Cook combined ingredients until they boil and thicken. Remove from heat. Add butter & rum. Stir until butter melts. Let cool to room temperature.
    10. Assembly:
  10. Slice off the top of each puff and fill the cavity with cream. Arrange puffs in the shape of a pyramid. Pour Coffee Rum Sauce over and serve.
