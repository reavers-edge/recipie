Buttermilk cheese biscuits

Back

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 Tbsp sugar
  • 2 Tbsp milk powder
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp parsley
  • 110 g butter
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2 cup (220 g) cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450f or 225c. Melt the butter, set aside.
  2. Combine flour, salt, sugar, milk powder, baking powder, and the garlic powder and parsley.
  3. Add the melted butter and buttermilk into the dry ingredients and use a rubber spatula to fold in the ingredients. Add the cheese and fold into the dough until combined.
  4. Drop 2 Tbsps worth of dough and flatten into a circle. Bake for 10-12 minutes until golden brown on the outside.
