Three Cup Chicken

Back

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs
  • pinch salt
  • pinch white pepper
  • 2 Tbsp cornflour
  • 2 Tbsp rapeseed oil
  • 6 cm ginger root
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 red chillies
  • 2 Tbsp shaoxing rice wine
  • 100 ml soy sauce, light
  • 100 ml sesame oil, toasted
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 2 bunches thai basil leaves
    • To serve
  • rice

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Peel and cut the [ginger root into large slices, crush the garlic clove but leave it whole and slice the red chilli.] Cut the [chicken thighs] in to bite sized pieces
    2. Recipe
  2. Put the chicken in a bowl, add the salt and ground white pepper and then dust with the cornflour. Mix well to coat and then set aside.
  3. Heat a wok over a high heat until smoking and add the rapeseed oil. Add the ginger, garlic and red chilli and toss for a few seconds to release the flavour.
  4. Add the chicken pieces to the pan and leave the chicken for 10 seconds without moving to sear and colour, then flip the thighs over.
  5. Pour in the Shaoxing rice wine (or dry sherry) and stir fry for 2-3 minutes on a high heat, or until the chicken is almost cooked.
  6. Add the light soy sauce, toasted sesame oil and sugar and cook for 5 minutes, or until the liquid has almost evaporated. The chicken should have a dark brown, slightly sticky shine. Add the thai basil leaves and toss through to wilt, then take off the heat and serve immediately with rice.
