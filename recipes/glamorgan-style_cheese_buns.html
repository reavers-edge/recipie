Glamorgan-style Cheese Buns

Back

Ingredients

    Dough Wet Ingredients
  • 220 g water
  • 10 g sugar
  • 7 g yeast
  • 1 (60 g) egg
    • note: total weight of egg + water should be 280g
    Dough Dry Ingredients
  • 400 g strong flour/li>
  • 10 g salt
    • Filling Ingredients
  • 180 g cheese
  • 20 g cheese powder
  • 95 g butter

Instructions

    Dough
  1. Mix the water, sugar and yeast, allow to proof for 5-10 minutes.
  2. Whisk the salt into the flour.
  3. Once the yeast mixture is foamy, beat in the egg, then add the wet ingredients to the dry and stir to form a ragged dough. The dough will likely seem fairly dry at this point.
  4. Knead the dough by hand or with a dough hook until smooth and elastic, about 5-8 minutes on speed 2. Dough should be somewhat sticky when everything is fully incorporated.
  5. Add a small amount of oil to the bowl and surface of the dough, then cover and let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
    6. Filling
  6. Mix the powdered cheese with the shredded cheese.
  7. Deflate the dough and turn out onto a work surface sprayed with water or rubbed with oil. Stretch or roll the dough into a rectangle of even thickness, around 14" x 20". The dough should not be less than 1 cm thick but not windowpane thin, it should still have enough thickness to proof up nicely later. Press out the edges to avoid a thick rim on the outside of the rectangle.
  8. Spread most of the butter across the dough sheet, going all the way to the edge. Sprinkle with most of the cheese mixture, reserving some for the next step.
  9. Fold the dough sheet in thirds along the long axis (picking up the short sides and folding them in). Spread the remaining butter and cheese on half of the top surface, then fold in half over the cheese, creating a total of six layers. Cover dough and allow to rest for a few minutes.
  10. Tuck the outer edge seams to the bottom of the dough and gently roll into a thick log shape, then cover and allow to rest for another few minutes. Press the log down into a thick rectangle, just enough to be able to cut it into even portions.
  11. Cut the dough into 12 pieces and place loosely in a greased muffin tin. Leave enough space in the tin for the bottoms to expand during proofing. Brush tops with any residual butter you may have left. Proof for 40-60 minutes, preheating oven to 350F when proofing looks almost complete.
  12. Bake at 175ºC, 160ºC fan, or 350ºF. until lightly browned, about 30 minutes. I recommend a tray on a lower rack to catch any butter that may drip down.
  13. Buns can be left to cool in the tin, but should be placed in a sealed container while they are still a bit warm, which will lightly steam them and keep them soft for longer.
