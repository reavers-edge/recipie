Nanaimo Bars

Back

Ingredients

    Bottom layer:
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 5 Tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 3/4 cups graham cracker crumbs
    • [about 12 biscuits]
  • 1 cup shredded coconut
  • 1/2 cup almonds, chopped
    • Middle layer:
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 40 ml heavy cream
  • 2 Tbsp custard powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cup icing sugar
    • Top layer:
  • 113 g chocolate
  • 2 Tbsp butter

Instructions

    Bottom layer:
  1. Melt the first 3 ingredients in top of a double boiler. Add egg and stir to cook and thicken. Remove from heat.
  2. Add graham cracker crumbs, coconut, and almonds. Mix well and press into a greased 8x8 inch pan.
    3. Middle layer:
  3. Cream together butter, cream, custard powder, and vanilla. Add icing sugar and mix until light. Spread over the bottom layer.
    4. Top layer:
  4. Melt chocolate and butter together. Cool, but while still liquid, spread over the middle layer. Chill until firm for 1 hour, then cut into squares.
