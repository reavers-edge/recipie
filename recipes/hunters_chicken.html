Hunter's Chicken

Back

Ingredients

  • 10 chicken breast
  • 20 bacon
  • 1/2 bottle BBQ sauce
  • 220 g cheese

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 200ºC, 185ºC fan, or 380ºF. and oil or line a baking tray.
  2. Place the chicken breasts on the tray and wrap two slices of bacon around each. Cook in the oven for 25 minutes. (You may want to flip the chicken over as it cooks so the bacon underneath can crisp.)
  3. After 25 minutes pour the barbecue sauce over the chicken, add the grated cheese and return to the oven for a further 5 minutes.
    • If freezing, don't add the cheese in step 3, and add it to the container when freezing. Later, defrost and cook for 30 mins.
