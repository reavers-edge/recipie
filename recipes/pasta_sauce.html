Pasta Sauce

Back

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 diced oniln
  • 4 minced garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp basil
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 800 g chopped tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup water
  • To serve pasta

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Dice the [onion] and [garlic].
    2. Recipe
  2. Heat the olive oil on high heat until it shimmers. Sauté the onions, stirring frequently, until they soften and shine, about 3 minutes. The onions should sizzle and hiss as they cook. Add the garlic, and stir to combine to prevent the garlic from burning. Cook for an additional 2 minutes.
  3. Add the basil, red pepper flakes, salt, and sugar. Stir to combine. Add the butter. Stir, cook for about a minute.
  4. Add 1/2 of the crushed tomatoes. Scrape the bottom of the pan to remove any stuck on bits. Reduce the heat to low. Add remaining tomatoes. Stir in the water. If the sauce seems thick, add additional water.
  5. Allow the sauce to simmer for 10 minutes or up to 1 hour. If simmering on the longer side, stir occasionally and add additional water as necessary to keep the sauce at the correct consistency.
  6. Mix with the pasta.
