Peanut Butter & Cornflake Chocolate Tiffin

Back

Ingredients

    Bar:
  • 200 g digestive biscuits
    • [about 14 biscuits]
  • 50 g cornflakes
  • 200 g chocolate
  • 50 g peanut butter
    • Topping:
  • 150 g chocolate

Instructions

    bar:
  1. Line a square brownie pan with non-stick baking paper or foil backed greaseproof paper.
  2. Whizz up digestive biscuits and cornflakes in a food processor or pop them in a large freezer bag and crush them with a rolling pin. Leave a bit of texture, you don't want it to be too fine.
  3. Melt the chocolate in a bowl with the peanut butter.
  4. In a large bowl mix together the biscuit mixture and melted chocolate together until well mixed, then press into the lined brownie pan. Chill in the fridge.
    5. Topping:
  5. Melt the second batch of chocolate and spread over the tiffin base and chill.
  6. Once set, remove from the pan and cut into squares. Store in an airtight container in the fridge.
