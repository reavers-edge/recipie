Almond Roca Bar

Back

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 cup almonds, chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a medium saucepan, combine sugar, butter, and water. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture reaches 300°F (150°C) on a candy thermometer.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in chopped almonds, vanilla extract, and salt. Pour the mixture onto the prepared baking sheet and spread it evenly.
  4. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until golden brown. Remove from oven and let it cool completely.
  5. Once cooled, melt the chocolate chips in a microwave-safe bowl or using a double boiler. Spread the melted chocolate over the almond roca layer.
  6. Allow the chocolate to set before cutting into bars. Store in an airtight container.
