Almond Biscotti

Back

Ingredients

    Dry ingredients
  • 340 g flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
    • Wet ingredients
  • 3 eggs
  • 4 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tsp almond extract
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla etract
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
    • Additions
  • 1 cup almonds, sliced (toasted)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 170ºC, 160ºC fan, or 325ºF.
  2. Whisk together the dry ingredients flour, baking powder, salt and sugars.
  3. In another bowl, whisk eggs. Add olive oil, extracts, and zest. Whisk lightly together.
  4. Add flour mixture to egg mixture; stir with a wooden spoon until just incorporated.
  5. Fold in the almonds (make sure they have cooled off before incorporating in the batter).
  6. Use a large serving spoon to scoop out the dough and place it on the parchment-lined cookie sheet. Add another scoop adjacent to the batter on the cookie sheet and keep going until two logs are formed. With damp fingers shape dough into a 3x14-inch log
  7. Bake for approximately 20 minutes (was 30 min) or until golden brown in colour and firm. Allow to cool slightly for about 10 minutes.
  8. Remove from baking sheet and transfer to a cutting board. Using a serrated knife, slice cookies at an angle about ½ - ¾ inch thick.
  9. Place slices back on the baking sheets and return them to the oven for about another 20 minutes (the longer they stay in the oven, the crispier they get). Turn them over at the halfway mark. Place on a wire rack to cool.
