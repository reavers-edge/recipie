Chocolate Banana Brownies

Back

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup chocolate
  • 2 bananas
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150 g flour

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 175ºC, 160ºC fan, or 350ºF.
  2. In a large bowl, melt the butter and white chocolate or chocolate in 30 second bursts, stirring each time until melted.
  3. Stir in the sugar. Mix in the mashed bananas.
  4. Add eggs, salt & vanilla and stir until well combined. Mix in the flour until completely incorporated.
  5. Spread batter into a 8" square pan and bake 15-20 minutes or until tester comes out clean. Cool completely and store covered.
