Thai Green Chicken

Back

Ingredients

  • 250 g jasmine rice
  • 125 g baby corn
  • 3 spring onions
  • 2 cm ginger root
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 100 g edamame beans, frozen
  • 100 g thai green curry paste
  • 1 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 400 ml chicken stock
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 4 chicken thighs
  • pinch salt
    • Garnish
  • lime wedges
  • fresh coriander
  • red chilli

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Chop the [baby corn and spring onions into 3cm pieces. Peel and finely grate the ginger root and garlic. — All into roasting tin]. Note chicken stock, dissolve with Thai green curry paste.
    2. Preparation
  2. Preheat the oven to 200C/180C Fan/Gas 6..
  3. Place the rice, baby corn, spring onion, ginger, garlic and edamame beans in a large baking dish or roasting tin.
  4. Spoon in 85g/3oz of the curry paste and then add the fish sauce, coconut milk, stock and honey. Stir all of the ingredients together well.
  5. Score each chicken a couple of times using a sharp knife. Spread the chicken with the remaining curry paste and sprinkle over a pinch of salt.
  6. Carefully lay the chicken on the top of the rice mixture - do not submerge the chicken as you want as much of the skin to crisp as possible.
  7. Bake in the oven for 30-35 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and crisp, and the rice has absorbed the liquid. The chicken is cooked through when the juices run clear with no trace of pink when the thickest part of the meat is pierced with a skewer. Remove from the oven and fluff up the rice with a fork.
    8. Preparation
  8. Serve immediately with lime wedges, a sprinkling of the chopped coriander and some red chilli slices, if using.
