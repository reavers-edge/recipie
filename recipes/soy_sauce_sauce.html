Soy Sauce Sauce

Back

Ingredients

  • 55 ml honey
  • 4 Tbsp soy sauce, dark
  • 2 Tbsp lime juice
  • 1 Tbsp sesame oil, toasted
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
    • or
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 15 g ginger root (optional)

Instructions

  1. Dice the garlic cloves and ginger if using.
  2. Mix all ingredients.
