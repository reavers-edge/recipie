Honey Sesame Chicken

Back

Ingredients

    Sauce
  • 2 Tbsp honey
  • 1/2 Tbsp rice wine vinegar
  • 2 tsp soy sauce, dark
  • 1 tsp sesame oil, toasted
  • 6 Tbsp water
    • Garnish
  • 4 chicken thighs
  • 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 80 g flour
  • 1/2 tsp sesame seeds, toasted
    • Garnish
  • rice
  • spring onions

Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Cut the chicken thighs into bite sized pieces.
    2. Sauce
  2. To make the sauce, whisk together all of the ingredients along with 6 tablespoons cold water in a jug or bowl. Set aside.
    3. Chicken
  3. Toss the chicken pieces, oil and salt together in a bowl. Beat and stir the egg into the bowl, then add the flour and stir with a metal spoon until the chicken is well coated.
  4. Heat enough oil to just cover the bottom of a large wok or frying pan over a medium–high heat. Once the oil is shimmering, add the chicken pieces in batches and fry for a few minutes on each side until golden and cooked through. Set the chicken aside to drain on a plate lined with kitchen paper.
  5. Discard all of the oil in the pan. Lower to a medium heat and return the chicken to the pan, heating through until it is sizzling. Add the sauce and cook for 3–4 minutes until the sauce has thickened and reduced to coat the chicken.
  6. Stir in the sesame seeds.
    7. Garnish
  7. Garnish with spring onions and serve with rice.
