Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Finely chop the [garlic]. [Scotch bonnet pepper and onion].
    2. Preparation
  2. In a large bowl, combine the allspice, thyme, cayenne pepper, black pepper, sage, nutmeg, cinnamon, salt, garlic and sugar.
  3. With a wire whisk, slowly add the olive oil, soy sauce, vinegar, orange and lime juice.
  4. Add the Scotch Bonnet pepper and onion, and put entire mixture in blender.
  5. IAdd the chicken, cover and marinate for at least an hour (the longer the better).
  6. Set oven to 200c. Bake for 35-40 minutes. Marinate after 15 minutes.
  7. Bring the leftover marinade to a boil...this will be your sauce when the chicken is cooked. Very important to BOIL the marinade.
  8. To serve, slice or chop the chicken into pieces and top with leftover (boiled) marinade as the sauce.
