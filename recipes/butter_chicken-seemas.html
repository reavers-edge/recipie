Instructions

    Preparation
  1. Crush the [garlic cloves and chillies, and grate the root ginger] - see paste. Finely chop the onion. Cut the chicken thighs into bite-sized pieces.
    2. Recipe\ Paaste
  2. To make the paste, mix together the garlic, ginger, chilli and oil in a small bowl.
    3. Chicken marinade
  3. To make the chicken marinade, in a large bowl mix the yoghurt, lemon juice and spices together with the salt, then stir in half of the garlic, ginger and chilli paste. Add the chicken and mix well. If you’re making this ahead, you can now cover and leave in the fridge until the next day. If not, carry on as below.
  4. Preheat the grill to its highest setting. Place the marinated chicken on a baking tray and grill until it’s charred, about 5–6 minutes on each side. It doesn’t need to cook through at this stage. Set aside..
    5. Curry
  5. Meanwhile, to make the curry, in a large saucepan melt the butter over a medium heat. Once it’s melted, turn dial to 3 and add the onion and cumin seeds along with the star anise, cinnamon stick and cloves, if using. Cook the onions for about 10 minutes, until they turn golden and caramelised.
  6. Add the rest of the garlic, ginger and chilli paste to the pan, along with the ground spices, salt and tomato purée. Cook for another 10 minutes, until the tomato purée separates from the oil.
  7. Add the tinned tomatoes and cook for 15–20 minutes, until the sauce thickens and the oil separates from the tomato mixture, creating a rich sauce.
  8. Add the grilled chicken, along with any cooking liquid. Stir in the double cream and add the dried fenugreek leaves, if using. Stir well and cook for about 5 minutes, until the chicken is fully cooked through.
  9. Squeeze in the juice of half a lemon, taste and adjust the seasoning if necessary.
    10. To serve
  10. Garnish the curry with fresh coriander and serve with rice and roti or naan bread.
