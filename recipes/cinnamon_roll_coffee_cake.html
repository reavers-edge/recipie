Cinnamon Roll Coffee Cake

Back

Ingredients

    Cake
  • 1/8 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup egg
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 80 g flour
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • pinch salt
  • 1/4 cup milk
    • Topping
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 Tbsp flour
  • 3/4 tsp cinnamon
    • Glaze
  • 1/2 cup icing sugar
  • 3 Tbsp milk
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

    Cake
  1. Preheat oven to 175ºC, 160ºC fan, or 350ºF.
  2. In a large bowl, mix all the cake ingredients together until well combined.
  3. Pour into a greased 8x8 pan.
    4. Topping
  4. Mix all the topping ingredients together in a small bowl until well combined.
  5. Spread evenly on the batter and swirl with a knife. Bake at 350 for 30-35 minutes.
    6. Glaze
  6. For the glaze, mix all the ingredients together in a small bowl until it is a consistency like pancake syrup.
  7. While warm drizzle the glaze over the cake and it will set into a harder glaze.
