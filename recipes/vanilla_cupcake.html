Vanilla Cupcake

Back

Ingredients

    Cupcake
  • 200 g butter
  • 200 g sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 190 g flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 Tbsp milk
    • Icing
  • 160 g butter
  • 300 g icing sugar
  • 1 Tbsp milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

    Cupcake
  1. Preheat the oven to gas 4, 180°C, fan 160°C. Line a 12-hole cupcake tin with paper cases.
  2. Put the butter and sugar in a large mixing bowl and beat with an electric whisk (or by hand with a wooden spoon) for 2-3 mins until pale and fluffy.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Add the vanilla extract and beat briefly to combine.
  4. Gently fold in the flour and just enough milk to make a smooth batter that drops easily off the spoon.
  5. Divide the batter between the cases, then bake for 20-25 mins or until a skewer inserted into the middle comes out clean. Transfer the cakes to a wire rack and leave to cool completely.
    6. Icing
  6. Meanwhile, make the buttercream icing. Put all the ingredients in a large mixing bowl and beat with an electric whisk for 4-5 mins until pale and fluffy.
  7. Swirl the icing over the cooled cupcakes to serve. Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
